01 Overview

Limitless By AI publishes short-form AI/SaaS-tool videos to YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram under the handle @limitlessbyai, using a companion content-automation app built for internal use only. This app publishes exclusively to our own accounts on those platforms — it is not a public tool, and it has no other users. This policy explains what data the app processes to do that, and what it deliberately does not collect.

02 What we process

To publish our own videos, the app stores OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram for our own connected accounts, along with basic upload metadata (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, scheduling times) for the videos we publish. These tokens are used only to call each platform's official publishing API on our behalf.

03 What we don't collect

Because the app has no viewers, followers, or third-party users of its own, it does not collect or process:

  • Personal data from anyone who watches, follows, comments on, or messages our channel — those interactions happen entirely on YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram, under each platform's own privacy policy.
  • Any other creator's account credentials, tokens, or content.
  • Payment, financial, or government ID information of any kind.

05 Storage & security

OAuth tokens and upload metadata are stored in credentials and workflow storage we control directly, are never sold or shared with any third party beyond the platform each token was issued by, and access is limited to the channel's operator.

06 Retention

We retain OAuth tokens for as long as the connected account remains active with our publishing app, and delete them once a platform is disconnected or a token is revoked. Upload metadata is retained for our own internal record-keeping of what we've published.

07 Children's privacy

Our app and this policy are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from children through it. Our published videos are general-audience content about AI and SaaS tools.

08 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the channel and its tooling evolve. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

09 Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via direct message to @limitlessbyai on YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram.
